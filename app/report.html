How to read this claim

  • Identified means the spine was read and a seller or catalogue page confirmed the book. Not identified means the spine could not be read well enough; no title is guessed.
  • Replacement is the price of a new copy today; used is a second-hand listing in the condition shown under it. Each price links to the page it was read on. Converted means the page priced it in another currency and it was converted at that day's ECB rate.
  • Totals add up only the lines with a confirmed price. Lines without one are counted as left out and listed under "What a person needs to check".
  • The picture on each line is what the camera saw; Seen in links open the full frames.
